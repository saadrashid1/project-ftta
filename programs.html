Whether you're picking up a paddle for the first time or preparing for your next tournament, we have a program built for you.

Beginners

A structured introduction to table tennis. Learn proper grip, stance, footwork, and the four basic strokes. Sessions are small-group focused with plenty of rally time and coach feedback.

Juniors

Age-appropriate coaching for young athletes aged 8–17. Focus on technique, discipline, and match play in a fun, supportive environment. Pathway to provincial and national competitions.

Adults & Competitive

High-intensity training for serious players. Tactical drills, multi-ball sessions, and regular match play. Ideal for club league players and tournament competitors looking to sharpen every aspect of their game.
